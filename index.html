    How to play
    1. Everyone starts on Start with 2 Hour Cards.
    2. On your turn, roll the die and move that many spaces. The space you land on gives or takes hours, moves you, makes you skip a turn, or lets you roll again.
    3. Memory Booster spaces draw a card. Good sleep helps memory, mood, and focus, so most of those cards reward you.
    4. If you run out of Hour Cards, keep playing. You can still earn more.
    5. The game ends as soon as one player reaches End. Count the hours: most sleep wins. Reaching End first doesn't win by itself.

    “Next player” is the player whose turn comes after yours. “Previous player” is the one before you. If the turn timer runs out, the game rolls for you so a big group keeps moving.

    Every space, explained
    How much sleep do people need?

    Average sleep per day drops as children grow up. Teenagers need more than most think.

    6 months old15 hr
    2 years old14 hr
    3–4 years old12 hr
    Teenagers8½–9 hr

    When people don't get enough, concentration, coordination, memory, and mood all suffer.
